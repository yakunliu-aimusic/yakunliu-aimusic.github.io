This study demonstrates a replicable workflow for immersive remote concert production.

Key contributions include:

- Proposing a dual-stage VR live-streaming architecture that fuses campus LAN and 5G transmission.
- Addressing latency and synchronization issues in cross-regional collaborative music performance.
- Integrating green screen matting, holographic projection, and 5.1 surround sound pickup.
- Validating the feasibility of campus network–5G convergence for live music applications.
