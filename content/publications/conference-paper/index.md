This paper constructs a multidimensional comparative framework for understanding traditional algorithmic composition approaches and their continuing relevance in contemporary computer music research.

Key contributions include:

- Categorizing traditional algorithmic composition into symbolic, optimization, complex systems, and statistical models.
- Detailing mechanisms from Markov chains to genetic algorithms.
- Evaluating algorithms according to structural control, stylistic simulation, innovation, interpretability, and human-computer collaboration.
- Clarifying paradigm shifts between traditional compositional thinking and algorithmic methodologies.
