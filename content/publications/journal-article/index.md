ChladniSonify proposes a reusable mapping framework for audio-visual artistic creation based on Chladni patterns.

Key contributions include:

- Presenting a novel real-time visual-acoustic mapping method for Chladni pattern-based new media art.
- Building a physically consistent paired dataset for visual-acoustic correspondence.
- Realizing high-precision, low-latency recognition through a CBAM-enhanced lightweight CNN.
- Developing a complete end-to-end real-time system for creative audio-visual performance.
