This Shenyang Conservatory of Music university-level research project explores the interdisciplinary frontiers of artificial intelligence and electronic music.

Yakun Liu served as core researcher under the guidance of Assoc. Prof. Dong Liu and Senior Eng. Hai Luan.

Key work included:

- Designing an interactive XR stage for live experimental performance.
- Implementing AI interaction logic for electroacoustic music contexts.
- Designing 5.1 surround sound spatialization workflows.
- Authoring the project research thesis.
- Producing original electroacoustic compositions as primary creative outputs.

Project period: November 2025 – November 2026, expected.
