**Qinmai Zhixu** is a Challenge Cup Entrepreneurship Competition project focused on reconstructing and preserving endangered Guqin melodies through deep learning-based temporal modeling.

Yakun Liu served as core researcher and lead developer. Advisors included Prof. Xiaolong Liu, Assoc. Prof. Dong Liu, Assoc. Prof. Chen Zhang, and Senior Eng. Hai Luan.

Key work included:

- Engineering an end-to-end GRU-based generative model pipeline.
- Curating a specialized Guqin MIDI dataset.
- Designing feature engineering workflows for style reconstruction.
- Training and deploying the model for real-time MIDI output.
- Framing the technical system within intangible heritage documentation and digital reconstruction of the Zhucheng Guqin school.

Project period: December 2025 – June 2026, expected.
